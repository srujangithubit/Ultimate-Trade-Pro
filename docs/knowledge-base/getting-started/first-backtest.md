# Your First Backtest

## Quick Start

1. Click **Backtesting** in the sidebar
2. Click **"+ New Session"**
3. Configure:
   - **Session name** — A descriptive name (e.g., "AAPL Q1 2024")
   - **Instrument** — The ticker symbol (e.g., AAPL)
   - **Asset class** — Stock, Forex, Crypto, Futures, Options, or Index
   - **Date range** — Start and end dates for historical data
   - **Starting balance** — Your virtual account balance
4. Click **"Create Session"**

## Using Playback

- **▶ Play** — Advance through candles
- **⏸ Pause** — Stop to analyze
- **Speed slider** — 1x to 10x playback
- **⏭ Skip** — Jump to next day

## Placing Trades

1. Pause at your desired entry point
2. Click **Buy** (long) or **Sell** (short)
3. Set quantity, stop loss, and take profit
4. Click **Confirm**

## What's Next?

After completing your backtest, check **Analytics** to review your overall performance, or assign a **Playbook** to track strategy-specific results.
