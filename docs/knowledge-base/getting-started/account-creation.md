# Account Creation

## Creating Your Account

1. Visit [https://app.tradingplatform.com](https://app.tradingplatform.com)
2. Click **"Sign Up"**
3. Enter your email address and a password (minimum 6 characters)
4. Optionally provide a display name
5. Click **"Create Account"**

## Email Verification

After signup, you'll receive a verification email. Click the link to verify your email address. Verification is required to:
- Connect broker accounts
- Export data
- Access premium features

**Didn't receive the email?**
- Check your spam/junk folder
- Go to Settings → Profile → Resend Verification Email
- Make sure you entered the correct email address

## Account Security

- **Password requirements:** Minimum 6 characters
- **Two-factor authentication:** Enable in Settings → Security for an extra layer of protection
- **Session management:** Active sessions are listed in Settings → Security; revoke any you don't recognize
