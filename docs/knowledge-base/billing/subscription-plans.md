# Upgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **"Upgrade Plan"**
3. Select your desired tier
4. Complete checkout via Stripe
5. Your new plan activates immediately

Prorated billing: You only pay the difference for the remaining days in your current period.

---

# Cancellation Policy

1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **"Cancel Subscription"**
3. Confirm cancellation

Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. After that, your account reverts to the Free tier. Your data is retained for 90 days — resubscribe anytime to restore access.

**No refunds** for partial billing periods. Annual plans can be canceled anytime; the remaining months are not refunded but access continues through the paid period.
