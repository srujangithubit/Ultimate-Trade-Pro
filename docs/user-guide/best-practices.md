# Trading Best Practices

Maximize your results with these proven trading and platform usage tips.

---

## 1. Build a System Before You Trade

- Define your **edge** — What pattern or inefficiency are you exploiting?
- Create a **playbook** with specific entry/exit rules
- **Backtest** your playbook across multiple instruments and time periods
- Only go live when your backtest shows consistent results

---

## 2. Risk Management First

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **Risk max 1-2% per trade** | Survive losing streaks |
| **Set stop losses on every trade** | Eliminate catastrophic losses |
| **Cap daily loss at 3-5%** | Protect against tilt/revenge trading |
| **Use position sizing formulas** | Consistency beats gut feeling |
| **Never average into losers** | Cut losses, let winners run |

---

## 3. Journal Every Trade

- **Log immediately** after the trade — memory fades fast
- Include **why** you took the trade, not just the numbers
- **Screenshot** your chart at entry and exit
- **Tag by setup** so you can analyze which setups work
- Review journal entries **weekly** to spot patterns

---

## 4. Use Analytics to Improve

- **Check win rate by setup** — Stop trading setups with < 40% win rate
- **Monitor profit factor** — If < 1.0, you're losing money overall
- **Track drawdown** — If > 15%, reduce position size
- **Compare time periods** — Are you improving month over month?
- **Identify your best time of day** — Focus trading hours accordingly

---

## 5. Effective Backtesting

- **Don't cheat** — Use candle-by-candle playback, don't peek ahead
- **Match real conditions** — Use realistic position sizes and include fees
- **Test enough data** — Minimum 100 trades for statistical significance
- **Test bear AND bull markets** — Your strategy must work in both
- **Document results** — Compare backtested performance to live trading

---

## 6. Broker Integration Tips

- **Sync daily** — Keep your journal up-to-date automatically
- **Reconcile regularly** — Cross-check synced trades with your broker statement
- **Use read-only API keys** when possible for security
- **Monitor sync logs** — Check for errors or missed trades

---

## 7. Platform Workflow

A suggested daily workflow for maximum effectiveness:

1. **Morning** — Review yesterday's trades in the journal
2. **Pre-market** — Check analytics for recent performance trends
3. **Market open** — Follow your playbook rules for entries
4. **During session** — Log trades and notes in real-time
5. **Market close** — Screenshot winning and losing trades
6. **Evening** — Run a quick backtest on any new ideas
7. **Weekly** — Deep analytics review; update playbooks if needed

---

## 8. Common Mistakes to Avoid

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| Trading without a plan | Create a playbook first |
| Ignoring stop losses | Always set stops before entry |
| Overtrading | Set a max trades per day rule |
| Revenge trading after losses | Use daily loss limits |
| Not reviewing trades | Schedule weekly journal reviews |
| Skipping backtesting | Test every new idea before going live |
| Risking too much per trade | Never exceed 2% account risk per trade |
